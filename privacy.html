Privacy Policy & Spam Act Compliance

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Sydney, Australia

1. Who We Are

Ai Health Check (aihealthcheck.com.au) is operated by Saas33 Pty Ltd, based in Sydney, NSW, Australia. Our designated public contact email is help@saas33.com.au.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect business contact information solely for the purpose of analyzing public AI visibility and providing requested reports:

  • Business details: Business name, industry, website, public Sydney trading address, and phone number.
  • Contact details: Owner or representative full name, business email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Public AI footprint: Public queries submitted to artificial intelligence engines and publicly available responses.

3. Strict Exclusions

In accordance with our strict data protection principles: No individual financial earnings, tax records, employee personal health data, or private consumer information is ever collected, processed, or shared in any report or database.

4. Spam Act 2003 Compliance

We strictly comply with the Australian Spam Act 2003. Any opt-in for marketing emails or weekly advice is completely optional and unticked by default. Reports generated during an in-person scan are never dispatched automatically to a business owner without explicit rep review and confirmation.

5. Single Source of Truth

All lead entries and check summaries are maintained within our secure, encrypted Supabase database (Sydney region, Row Level Security enabled). Third-party spreadsheets are strictly read-only mirrors.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data or wish to have your details removed, please contact our privacy officer at help@saas33.com.au.
